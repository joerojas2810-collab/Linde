## Prompt exacto

Input image 1 caminata.png is identity and pixel detail authority (teal tousled hair, tan skin, turquoise tunic, dark navy pants, brown boots, large head, body about 27 native pixels high). Input image 2 herramienta.png is pose/scale authority; no tool or weapon is needed for this new sheet. Make a single landscape TRANSPARENT PNG arranged as a precise 5 COLUMN x 4 ROW grid, equally spaced cells, with empty transparent margins between all 20 characters. Each cell is conceptually 48x48 native pixels; artwork must stay extremely simple low resolution (head about 11 pixels wide, standing figure 26-27 pixels high), with hard pixel edges at integer nearest-neighbor enlargement, NO added fine detail. Columns left to right: 0 recoil upon being hurt (small backward lean, knees flexed, arms near torso); 1 recovering from hurt (upright but shoulders slumped, same identity); 2 falling onto knees (crouched down, lowered head, no injury effects); 3 lying down fallen (body horizontal on ground, resting head, same clothing); 4 sleeping (lying peacefully, eyes closed, hands resting, no bed, no blanket, no symbols). Rows top to bottom: facing FRONT toward viewer, facing RIGHT, facing BACK away from viewer, facing LEFT. Even fallen and sleeping characters retain orientation of head/face of their row. No walking, no attack, no hacha, no sword, no shield, no helmets, no armor, no impact effects, no blood, no floating stars, no letters, no labels, no UI, no floor, no background, no soft shadows. Preserve simple dark outline and existing flat 2-3 tones per material. Do NOT change character proportions or invent a new person. Same size head across all 20 poses, lying body must not be shrunk relative to upright body. Keep every sprite inside its own cell and isolated by clear transparency. This master sheet will be converted to exact native pixels and separated into editable semantic layers by a technical art pipeline.
