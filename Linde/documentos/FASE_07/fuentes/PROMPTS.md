Se usó la herramienta integrada image_gen, sin API externa. Tres atlas nuevos; el resto se reutiliza o adapta en Godot. Las referencias aprobadas se conservaron en arte/viajero071 dentro de la copia jugable.

## A · cinco estilos de cabello

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: production pixel-art HAIR-ONLY atlas, transparent background. Reference 1 is approved orange hair overlay; reference 2 shows approved character proportions and four directions. Create ONE organized atlas, exactly FOUR columns and FIVE rows, 20 separate hair overlays. Each cell has generous equal empty padding, aligned head center, no text or grid lines. Columns strictly: FRONT, facing RIGHT profile, BACK of head, facing LEFT profile. Rows strictly: 1 rounded chin-length BOB (Melena, longer than medium hair reference); 2 compact tied-back BUN (Recogido/Moño); 3 short TOUSLED hair; 4 short hair with part on character's physical LEFT; 5 short hair with part on character's physical RIGHT. Copper orange hair, dark brown outlines, amber highlights, discrete crisp square pixel clusters, limited palette, same detailed 64-pixel-wide head scale/proportions as reference. Deliver ONLY the detachable hair pieces, NO skin, face, eyes, ears, body, mannequin, weapons, lettering, shadows, checkers or white background. The front/profile eye area is an empty transparent opening; do not draw eyes. Back hair is solid scalp cover. Each hairstyle must be coherent in all four directions and distinct from other rows. Preserve genuinely transparent alpha everywhere outside hair and in face openings. No stray pixels, smooth vector shapes or anti-aliased painting.

## C · tres familias de equipo

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: ONE modular equipment atlas for the referenced pixel-art traveler. Exactly FOUR columns and THREE rows, twelve evenly spaced outfits. Columns FRONT, RIGHT profile, BACK, LEFT profile. Rows LIGHT leather armor with narrow straps and leather cap; MEDIUM blue-gray chainmail with small shoulder plates and open steel helmet; HEAVY pale steel plate armor with broad articulated shoulder plates and open steel helmet. All outfits have matching gloves, trousers/greaves and boots, visible belt space. Match the reference traveler's EXACT large-head slim-body proportions, stance and clothing size, same native pixel cluster detail, dark brown outlines, flat finite palette and crisp square pixels. Keep each outfit the same total size and feet on aligned cell baselines with equal padding. Important: output ONLY EQUIPMENT OVERLAYS positioned as if worn by reference traveler; no head, no face, no eyes, skin, hair or blue base shirt. Helmet face aperture is fully transparent and eyes/ears remain free; torso does not include hands or arms of skin, only matching sleeve/gauntlet equipment. Transparent gaps between boots and below arms. No weapons or shield, no cloak, no lettering, no panels, no white fill/checkerboard. Genuine transparent background. Steel must look silver/blue-gray, leather brown, belt buckle gold. Strong readable light/medium/heavy silhouette differences. Outfits are detachable overlay assets, not new characters.
